Restaurant content feed — Owner’s upload photos/stories, customers scroll and follow restaurants they like (like an Instagram feed, but restaurant-only).2. Customer-generated content — Customers post their own food pics + reviews, rated across food quality, restaurant ambiance, and presentation separately (not just one star rating).3. Location switching — Defaults to showing everything ("all country"), with a city filter the person can switch manually. You'll want to think about whether this is a dropdown/search, or auto-detected via GPS with manual override.4. QR-to-menu — Scan table QR → see that restaurant's actual menu, where every dish has the real uploaded photo next to its name/price. This turns the menu into proof, not just a description.User Roles & Access Control Owner: Full access to everything, especially Home (Analytics), User Management, and Add Expenses.Manager: Access to daily operations, Table Management, Billing, Load Menu, and Online Orders. Restricted from high-level financial profit history unless permitted by the owner.Reception / Cashier: Access to Billing, Table Management, and Online Orders. Can log out easily at shift change
